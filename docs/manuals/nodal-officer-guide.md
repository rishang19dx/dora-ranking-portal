# User Manual: Department Nodal Officer

You provide your department's ranking data and the documents that prove it. There is no account or password to remember: you work from the link DORA mails you.

## 1. Your upload link

- The mail from DORA contains a link like `https://arp.iitmandi.ac.in/portal/…`.
- It opens **only your section's forms for this request**, and only until the deadline.
- **Do not forward it.** Anyone with the link can enter data in your name.
- If DORA sends a reminder, **use the newest mail**. Older links stop working.

## 2. Filling the forms

1. Open the link. **NIRF Form Completion Progress** shows what is outstanding.
2. Fill each section. Work is saved as a draft, so you can stop and come back with the same link.
3. Impossible values (a negative count, a percentage above 100) are rejected with a message beside the field.
4. Where your department submitted last cycle, the previously approved value and the change are shown beside the field. A large change is fine if it is real, but expect DORA to ask about it.
5. If a section does not apply to your department, mark it **Not applicable**.

### Large tables: use the Excel template

For record lists such as faculty or sponsored projects:
1. **Download template** for that section.
2. Fill it offline without changing the column headings.
3. **Import**. Valid rows are saved; rows with problems are listed with the row number and reason.

## 3. Proofs

Where a field shows **Proof required**, upload the supporting document (PDF, Excel, Word or CSV, up to 25 MB) under **Documentary Evidence**. A sanction letter for a grant figure is a typical example. Uploading a corrected file keeps the old version on record.

## 4. Submitting

Press **Submit** on each section when it is complete. A submitted section is read-only while DORA reviews it.

## 5. Corrections

If DORA asks for a correction, you receive a mail with their comment. Open the link, fix the values (or explain why they are right), and submit again. Your section is final once DORA approves it.

## 6. My Analytics (department heads with an account)

Department heads who sign in see their own department's contribution (research funding, placements and similar) against anonymised institute benchmarks.

## 7. Help

Contact the DORA office first: the reply-to address on the request mail. Problems with the link itself (expired, or it will not open) are fixed by DORA re-issuing it.
