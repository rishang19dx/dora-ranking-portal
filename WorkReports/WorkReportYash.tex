\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt]

\item \textbf{Configuration Management:} Documented the secret placeholder in \texttt{.env.example} to ensure zero leakage of production credentials into source control.
\item \textbf{Validation:} Confirmed successful compilation of Next.js production routes and validated Python AST syntax for \texttt{app.py} and \texttt{scraper.py}.
\end{itemize}
