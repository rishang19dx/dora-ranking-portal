# User Manual: Director and Leadership

You have read-only access to every dashboard and report, and you are the signatory for the go/no-go decision.

## Executive Dashboard

Sign in (**Admin login**) and open **Dashboard**:

- **Rank trajectory**: IIT Mandi's NIRF rank over the selected years.
- **Gap to best peer** and **Against our own history**: the parameters where the institute trails second-generation IITs or its own average.
- **Department contribution**: which departments drive research funding and placements.
- **Collection bottlenecks**: sections holding up the current cycle.

Use **View cycle** to switch the cycle year.

## Boardroom briefing

From the cycle page, download the **briefing PDF**. It covers submission statistics, the discrepancies the system caught and how they were resolved, and scenario-based outlook figures. Projections are estimates, not predictions of the published rank.

## Approving the final submission

Before anyone uploads to the NIRF portal, review the DCS export and the briefing. The upload itself is done manually by the authorised signatory, as the agencies require.

## Go / no-go (Phase 4)

On **Go-Live Readiness → Sign-off**:
1. Read the **Readiness** tab. Every gate shows its live evidence.
2. Record **Go / no-go decision** with your name and designation.
3. Going live while the system recommends NO-GO is allowed, but a written justification is required and kept with the decision.

Sign-offs cannot be edited or deleted. A changed decision is recorded as a new sign-off.
